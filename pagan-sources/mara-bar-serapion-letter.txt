Mara bar Serapion - Letter to His Son
Translation: Public domain (from Cureton and later renderings)
Source: British Museum manuscript, Syriac letter of Mara bar Serapion

What else can we say, when the wise are forcibly dragged off by force, their wisdom is taken captive by insults, and their minds are oppressed and without defense? What advantage did the Athenians gain by putting Socrates to death, seeing that they received as retribution for it famine and pestilence? Or the people of Samos by the burning of Pythagoras, seeing that in one hour the whole of their country was covered with sand? Or the Jews by the death of their wise king, seeing that from that very time their kingdom was taken away from them? For with justice did God render the Jews recompense, because they had executed their wise king. For the king did not die for good, as Plato also said of Socrates. But this man, after he had been put to death, lived on in the teaching which he had given.
