The Passion of Perpetua and Felicitas
Translation: Public domain (Roberts / traditional English renderings)
Source: The Martyrdom of Perpetua and Felicitas

If ancient examples of faith, which both testified the grace of God and wrought the edification of man, have to this day been set forth in writing, that by their reading, God may be glorified and man strengthened; why should not new instances be also collected, that shall serve equally to both these ends? For these too will one day become ancient and needful for posterity, even though in their own present time they are esteemed of less authority, because there is a greater reverence for antiquity.

But let those who would restrict the power of the one Spirit to times and seasons look to this: the more recent events should be considered the greater, being later than those of old, and this is a consequence of the superabundance of grace promised for the last days of the world. For "In the last days, saith the Lord, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh; and their sons and their daughters shall prophesy. And upon my servants and upon my handmaidens will I pour out of my Spirit; and the young men shall see visions, and the old men shall dream dreams."
