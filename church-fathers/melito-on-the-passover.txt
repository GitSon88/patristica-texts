Melito of Sardis - On the Passover (Peri Pascha)
Translation: Public domain rendering based on the standard English versions of the recovered text
Source: Melito of Sardis, On Pascha (from the Bodmer and other papyri; public domain translations)

This is the one who rescued us from slavery into freedom, from darkness into light, from death into life, from tyranny into an eternal kingdom, who made us a new priesthood, a people chosen to be his own forever.

This is the passover of our salvation: this is the one who in many people endured many things. This is the one who was murdered in Abel, tied up in Isaac, exiled in Jacob, sold in Joseph, exposed in Moses, slaughtered in the lamb, hunted down in David, dishonoured in the prophets.

This is the one who was made flesh in a virgin, who was hung on a tree, who was buried in the earth, who was raised from the dead, who was taken up to the heights of heaven.

This is the lamb that was slain; this is the lamb that was silent; this is the one who was born of Mary the fair ewe; this is the one who was taken from the flock and dragged to slaughter and killed towards evening and buried at night; who on the tree was not broken, in the earth was not dissolved, who rose from the dead and raised up man from the depths of the grave.
